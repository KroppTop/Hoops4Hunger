Tournament Structure

Two Days of Tournament Hoops

Qualifiers start April 10 at the Boys & Girls Club, leading to Championship Saturday on April 17 at St. John Gym. Entry requires one bag of food per team.

April 10

Day 1: Qualifier Round

April 10 at Boys & Girls Club of Tri-County. Double elimination half-court timed games. The top twelve squads advance directly to Championship Saturday.

April 17

Day 2: Championship Saturday

April 17 at St. John Lutheran School Gym. Full-court single elimination tournament for qualifying teams competing for top cash prizes and trophies.

Entry Fee

1,000 Lbs Food Objective

One paper bag of nonperishable food items per squad collected at check-in. All donations go directly to support families through the Berlin Food Pantry.
