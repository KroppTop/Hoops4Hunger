Our mission

Basketball is a team sport. So is ending hunger.

Too many families in our area don't know where their next meal is coming from. Hoops4Hunger turns a day of friendly competition into real food for real people.

Every team entry fee, every donated can and every dollar raised goes to our local food bank partners, so the food stays right here in the neighborhood.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Who can play?

Everyone! We have youth, adult and open divisions so players of every age and skill level can join in.

How many players are on a team?

Three on the court, with up to one substitute, so register teams of three or four.

Where does the money go?

All proceeds and food donations go directly to our local food bank partners.

What food should I bring?

Non-perishable, unexpired items: canned vegetables, soups, beans and tuna, plus pasta, rice, peanut butter and cereal. Please no glass jars.

Can I volunteer?

Absolutely. We need scorekeepers, refs, check-in help and food sorters. Reach out using the contact link below.
